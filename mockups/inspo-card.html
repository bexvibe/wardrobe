Inspo on the outfit card

Mock-up only, nothing is wired. Try it: tap Add, pick a source, tap a thumbnail, remove it, then Undo.

Where it sits

At the bottom of the expanded card, under the pieces. The pieces are the outfit; inspo is about the outfit, so it comes after them and doesn't push them down.

Adding

  • Add inspo is always on screen: a round + at the top right of the section once there is any inspo, and the big + in the middle while there is none, the same as the app's other empty pages.
  • It opens the app's own dialog (the one used for confirmations and naming), with two choices.
  • Photo or screenshot opens the phone's own chooser, exactly as adding a photo to a piece does today, so camera and camera roll come from the phone with nothing for us to build.
  • Paste a link asks for one line of text in a dialog shaped like the app's naming prompt. Any link works: a Pinterest pin, an Instagram post, a shop page, or a direct image. An image link is saved as it is; a page link saves the page's preview picture. If there is none, a plain tile shows the site name.

Removing

  • Tap a thumbnail to see it larger, with Open original and Remove.
  • Remove acts at once and offers Undo for a few seconds. No confirm dialog. It's a soft delete, like the rest of the app.

Search Pinterest

A plain link to pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=…, with the query built from the pieces. On a phone with the Pinterest app, the link usually opens the app; otherwise it opens in the browser. The app can't know when you come back with a pin, which is why Add is always one tap away.

Showing them

All inspo shows at once in a three-column grid that wraps, so nothing sits off the edge of a scroll. Tapping a tile opens it larger.
