Inspo on the outfit card

Mock-up only, nothing is wired. Try it: tap Add, pick a source, tap a thumbnail, remove it, then Undo.

Where it sits

At the bottom of the expanded card, under the pieces. The pieces are the outfit; inspo is about the outfit, so it comes after them and doesn't push them down.

Adding

  • Take photo, Camera roll and Paste a link live in one sheet behind a single Add tile, so the card stays quiet.
  • Camera and roll are plain file inputs (capture for camera), so they work on iOS and Android with no permissions prompt of our own.
  • Link adds a thumbnail from the page's preview image. If none can be fetched, a plain tile shows the site name.

Removing

  • Tap a thumbnail to see it larger, with Open original and Remove.
  • Remove acts at once and offers Undo for a few seconds. No confirm dialog. It's a soft delete, like the rest of the app.

Search Pinterest

A plain link to pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=…, with the query built from the pieces. On a phone with the Pinterest app, the link usually opens the app; otherwise it opens in the browser. The app can't know when you come back with a pin, which is why Add is always one tap away.

Open question

Tiles are a horizontal strip so many pins don't make the card long. A 2-column grid would show more at once; say if you'd prefer that.
