Outfits page

The shuffle card

Slots you can fill, in the floating card, with back and forward arrows on the outfit and Shuffle and the heart in the bar. Every phone is live. Tap the arrows, or drag the outfit sideways. Tap a slot to choose a piece. Tap outside the card to close it.

How it behaves

Each control, in one line

  • 1Arrows.Back and forward step through outfits you have already seen, so a good one is never lost. Forward on the newest outfit deals a new one. Back is dimmed on the first.
  • 2Slots.A solid slot is chosen and locked. A faded slot is dealt for you, and says Choose. Tap either to open the picker. The padlock unlocks it.
  • 3Tags.Narrow what is dealt to pieces with that tag. Choosing a tag restarts the run of outfits.
  • 4Shuffle.Deals a new outfit around your locked pieces. It works the same whatever the arrows are showing, and the wand keeps the app's one purple.
  • 5Heart.Faves the outfit on screen, the same heart as everywhere else in the app.
  • 6Close.The cross, or a tap outside the card. The page's hero becomes the outfit you stopped on.
On FavesNo locks or picker, because a fave is a finished outfit. The arrows walk through your faves, and the chips narrow them by fave level.
Arrows and swipeDragging the outfit sideways does the same as the arrows, so the arrows are for people who do not discover the gesture.
Open questionThe arrows sit over the outfit's edges, so they can touch a piece at the sides. If that bothers you, they could move into the bar on either side of Shuffle. It costs the outfit a little less room but keeps the image clean.
