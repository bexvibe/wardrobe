Tonal button colour

Today the quieter button on a page's bar is a flat grey (#D5D2CC), close to the page behind it, so it reads as disabled. Five options, each shown on the outfit page's bar, and on the three buttons that use it: Edit accessories, Edit capsule and Open original. Mock-up only.

How I'd choose

  • The app's rule is that colour comes from the clothes, and the one hue (purple) is spent on Shuffle. So a tonal colour should be muted and light, a tint rather than a colour, or it will fight the cut-outs.
  • Sage is my pick: it's the colour that sits best beside most clothes, and it's far enough from the purple to avoid competing with Shuffle.
  • Lavender mist is the safest if you want it to feel related to Shuffle, but it blurs the line between "the main thing" and "an option".
  • Only the tonal button changes: Back stays the off-white pill and primary actions stay black.
