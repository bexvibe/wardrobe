Inspo page

Mock-up only, nothing is wired. Try it: tap the first tile and scroll the card up, then New inspo and walk the steps. Toggle Empty for the first-visit state.

In the nav

  • Inspo is a fourth plain destination at the end of the bottom bar, after Capsules. It uses the same .bottom-btn, same active wash. The bar's own comment already plans for four destinations on a 390px phone, so no change to padding is needed beyond a couple of px (shown at 11px here instead of 13px).
  • Like Capsules, it only appears once add-inspo.sql has been run (inspoAvailable).

The page

  • Same page head as every other page: Inspo on the left, New inspo on the right, the same button as New piece / New capsule.
  • Every inspo you've added on any outfit, newest first, in the app's existing inspo tile (3:4, cover, source chip bottom-left). Two columns instead of the outfit page's three, because here the images are the page. The Wardrobe gallery is also two columns.
  • A small count badge on a tile when it's saved to more than one outfit.
  • Empty state is the app's standard one: emoji, line, round-ish button, no box.

The popup is now a full page

  • It uses the app's outfit-page pattern, not a bottom sheet: a card rises over the blurred page, and scrolling it up turns it into the full page (same 40px rule as the outfit page). Scroll back to the top and it's a card again; tap the dimmed area above it to close.
  • The image is large at the top. Under it, Saved to N outfits shows the outfits as the app's own outfit cards, two side by side, laid out as worn. Tap a card to open that outfit's page.
  • The foot is the app's page bar: Back as its own pill, then Open original and Remove as pills of their own.

New inspo from here

On an outfit's page it's obvious where inspo goes. Up here it isn't, so after choosing the photo or link there's one extra step: Save to, a full page of outfit cards side by side. Tap cards to tick them (outlined, filled when on), then Save. Photo and link choice stay as the app's small add sheets, since those only pick a source.
