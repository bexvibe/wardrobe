Start from the basics

A new user opens the wardrobe, finds it empty, and can fill it from a curated set of cutouts in under a minute, on their phone, without photographing a thing. Picture shapes here are placeholders: the real tiles use the generated cutouts. Everything else uses the app's own tokens, bars and sheet rules (staged picker with Cancel and a filling primary, grips only where the sheet answers a drag).

Decisions I'd like your call on

  1. Colour on the tile or a second step? Shown: coloured dots under each tile, one tap per colour. The alternative is tapping a tile to open a colour strip, which is calmer but adds a tap per item.
  2. “Select the essentials” button. One tap ticks about 12 everyday pieces (white and black tee, dark and black jeans, a hoodie, sneakers, a cap...). Fastest start, but it assumes a lot. Keep, or drop?
  3. Category row at the top or the bottom. Shown at the top, as the other pickers do it. The thumb rule says touch controls belong low, so a row just above the bar is the alternative.
  4. “Stock” tag on wardrobe tiles. Shown, so placeholders are honest and easy to find. It could instead appear only on the piece sheet.
