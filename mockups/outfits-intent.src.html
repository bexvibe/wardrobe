Outfits page

Start from what you know

You usually know one or two pieces, or a tag like work or dinner, before you open the app. These three options make telling the app that the fastest thing on the page, and let Shuffle fill in the rest. Every phone is live and starts with a realistic day already set. Tap a tag, add or remove a piece, and shuffle.

Your input comes firstThe app guesses only when you have nothing to say. If you know the jacket or the occasion, that leads.
Pieces become locksA piece you choose is a locked piece. One idea instead of two: Shuffle deals around whatever you pinned.
Tags narrow the dealTags are the same tags you already have. Tap one and only matching pieces are dealt. Several tags widen it.
Recents save repeat tapsPieces you used lately are first in every list, so a repeat week costs one tap.
Option 1

Tags and pieces in the card

The shuffle card from last time, with the two things you know directly under the outfit. Tags first, then a strip of your pieces with the recent ones at the front. Tap a piece to pin it, and it locks into its slot.

GoodEverything is in one place: the outfit, what you told it, and Shuffle. It builds on the card you already liked.
Trade-offThe card gets busier, and the outfit gets smaller to make room. You have to open the card before you can say anything.
Best forMornings where you know one piece and want to see it with different things.
Option 2

A Today bar on the page

No popup at all. A bar under the title where you set the day: tag chips, and pieces you have pinned with an Add a piece button. The hero and the grid respond straight away, so the page itself stops being random.

GoodThe fastest way in: no extra tap to open anything. What you set is visible all the time, so you always know why the page looks as it does.
Trade-offIt is a second Filters, and it uses the top of the page. It would need to replace the Filters pill for tags and pieces, or the two will compete.
Best forPeople who use the page all day and change their mind. The state is always there.
Option 3

Slots you can fill

The card shows four slots: jacket, top, bottom and shoes. A slot you have chosen is solid and locked. A slot you have not chosen is faded and says Choose. Tap any slot to open a picker for that slot, with recents first and your tags applied. Shuffle deals only the faded slots.

GoodIt matches how you actually decide: "jacket is the Waverly, shoes are the Blundstones, sort out the rest". Choosing a piece and locking it are one action.
GoodThe picker only lists pieces that fit the slot, so it is much shorter than scrolling the wardrobe.
Trade-offMost to build, and a piece takes two taps (slot, then piece) instead of one.
Best forKnowing two or three pieces for the day.
Notes

What carries over and what is open

TagsThe mockup uses made-up tags (Work, Dinner, Weekend, Beach). In the app these are your existing tags.
RecentsNeeds a small remembered list of the last pieces and tags you used. It can live on the device and does not need a new column.
LensesNew in, Overlooked and Remix are left out here. They could sit as a last chip for the days you know nothing.
FavesLeft out too. Faves is already a short list you pick from, so the problem is mostly on All.

Photos are real cut-outs from the wardrobe. Tags, recents and outfits are made up for the mockup.
