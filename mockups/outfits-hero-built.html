Worth knowing

What changes, and what to look at

Two titlesThe hero is titled "Try this" and the grid "More combos". Both are placeholders, so tell me if you want different words. Shuffle sits in the hero's title row, because on a normal phone the floating Filters pill would otherwise cover a Shuffle button placed on the card.
Shuffle belongs to the heroOn All it deals only the hero. The grid stays put, and the floating Shuffle is gone from this page. On Faves the floating Shuffle stays, as it reorders what you kept.
Only these things are newThe spaces in the hero, the padlock on each, tapping a space to open Filters on it, and Shuffle moving onto the hero. Everything else on the page is as it is today.
The hero is no longer a buttonToday, tapping the hero opens that outfit's page. Now a tap opens Filters, so the way into an outfit's page is the grid below. Tell me if you want a way to keep it on the hero.
The heart overlaps a cornerThe fave heart sits where it does today, top right, so it can touch the top space. I can nudge it if it looks tight to you.
None is on jacket and jumper onlyTop and bottom (or the dress) always have a piece, as the app already requires.
