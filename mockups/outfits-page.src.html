Outfits page

Hero on the page, filters as they are

No popup for the featured outfit. It sits at the top of the page with back and forward arrows on the image, a padlock on every piece, and Shuffle and the heart under it. Choosing pieces and tags uses the filters sheet you already have. Every phone is live. Tap the arrows or the padlocks. Tap a piece to open Filters at that slot.

Locking is filteringA padlock narrows that slot to the one piece, exactly like selecting a single piece in Filters. So the Filters badge counts your locks, and the two never disagree.
One layerThe hero is the page, so the only thing that ever sits over it is the filters sheet.
Tapping a piece goes to its slotThe sheet opens with that slot's pieces already showing, instead of making you find the category.
Nothing new to learnThe sheet, the pill and the "See N outfits" button are the ones in the app today.
How it behaves

Each control, in one line

  • 1Arrows.Step back and forward through outfits you have seen. Forward on the newest deals a new one. Dragging the outfit sideways does the same.
  • 2Padlock.Keeps the piece that is showing. It is the same as selecting that one piece in Filters, so the Filters badge goes up by one.
  • 3Tap a piece.Opens Filters at that piece's slot, with its pieces showing. Selecting several narrows the slot to those. Selecting one is a lock.
  • 4Shuffle and heart.Shuffle deals around everything you have locked or filtered. The heart faves the outfit on screen.
  • 5Filters pill.Where tags live, unchanged. Its badge counts tags plus pieces, and the button at the bottom of the sheet says how many outfits that leaves.
FavesSame hero and arrows. No padlocks, since a fave is a finished outfit, and the button reads Show another. Filters works as it does today.
CostChoosing a piece takes a trip into the sheet, which is the layer the card version was trying to avoid. The difference is that it is the layer the app already has, and the hero stays one tap behind it.
Wider effectBecause a lock is a filter, the grid below follows it too. Lock the Waverly jacket and every outfit in the grid has it.

Photos are real cut-outs from the wardrobe. Tags and outfits are made up for the mockup. The count in the sheet is worked out from the pieces shown, so it is approximate.
