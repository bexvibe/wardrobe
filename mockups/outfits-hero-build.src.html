Outfits page, All

What I'll build: the hero on All

The featured outfit at the top of All, with a space for each of jacket, jumper, top and bottom. Tap a space to open Filters at that category. Lock a space to keep it. None is a choice for the layers you can go without. It starts on a random outfit and Shuffle deals a new one around anything you've locked. No shoes, no tags. Every phone is live: tap a space, a padlock, Shuffle, or Filters.

The plan

What exists, what's new, what I need from you

Already in the appThe hero card and its Shuffle. The filters sheet with a category row and piece grid. A "none" choice for jackets and jumpers. Pinning one piece as a filter. The reason line above the hero is new, but the rest of this page is today's.
NewFour labelled spaces in the hero instead of one picture. A padlock on each. Tapping a space opens Filters at that category. An empty jacket or jumper shows as a dashed space you can lock as none. A reason line above the outfit.
A lock is a pinned pieceIt sets the same filter as choosing one piece in the sheet, so Filters' badge counts it, Clear removes it, and the grid below follows. Nothing new is stored.
ShuffleDeals a new outfit from whatever the filters leave. Locked spaces stay, because they are the filters.
