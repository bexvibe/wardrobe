Back in the hero popup

Today Back closes the popup. Because Shuffle is right beside it, it reads as “undo my last shuffle”. Four ways to meet that expectation; the footer is the part that changes.

A · Back steps back literal

×
‹ Back✦ Shuffle

Back goes to the previous shuffle, as many times as you shuffled. Close moves to a × in the corner (and tapping outside). With no history Back is greyed.

B · Undo arrow + Done split

Done↶✦ Shuffle

Back becomes “Done” so it no longer promises history. Undo is its own icon next to Shuffle, greyed until you've shuffled. Clearest, but two controls to learn.

C · History stepper browse

×
‹✦ Shuffle›

‹ › move through every outfit you've dealt, Shuffle deals a new one at the end. You can go forward again after stepping back. Most powerful, busiest.

D · Back, then Done adaptive

×
‹ Back✦ Shuffle

After shuffling, Back steps to the previous outfit. On the first outfit (nothing to go back to) the same button reads “Done” and closes. × always closes. Phone back gesture does the same as Back.

Recommendation: D. It keeps today's footer and fixes the surprise: Back does what it implies while there is something to go back to, and falls through to closing when there isn't. The corner × and tap-outside give a constant, unambiguous way out. Phone back follows the footer. A is the same without the fall-through; B and C add controls this popup doesn't have room for.
