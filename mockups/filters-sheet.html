Filters as a bottom sheet

The Filters button raises a sheet that works like the app's other sheets. It starts as tall as its content and grows up to the height of the screen when it needs to. The one open question is how you pick pieces from inside it, so the phone below has three versions to try. Everything else is the same in all three.

  • No sheet on a sheetChoosing pieces happens inside the Filters sheet. The separate "Choose tops" picker goes away.
  • Thumb reach"See 1,284 outfits" is docked at the bottom edge, with Clear all quiet beside it. Nothing you need is in the top corners.
  • Same rules as the other sheetsGrip, 85–92% height, dimmed page, rounded top, Cancel-style quiet button on the left and the main action filling the right.
  • "See X outfits" is how you hide filtersThe count updates as you tap. There is no separate Hide button.
  • Swipe down to closeTry it on the grip or title. Same distance and flick speed as the current sheets.
  • Floats above Shuffle and the pillThe sheet covers both, and they ease down behind it.

To confirm before building

  • Which option for picking pieces? I'd build C. It's one tap between categories, and it reuses the tab rail from the outfit builder.
  • Dimmed page behind the sheet. Shown dimmed, like every other sheet. The old panel let you watch results change above it. A tall sheet covers most of that, so the count on the button now carries it. The alternative is no dim, and a shorter sheet.
  • Clear all lives in the footer, on the left and quiet, and only appears when a filter is on. The old position was the top right of the panel.
  • Everything applies live, like the current piece picker. There is no Save or Cancel, so the footer stays one main button.
  • On Faves, the same sheet says "See 12 saved outfits". Is that the wording you want?
