Outfits page

Shuffle and locks in a popup

The same idea as before, with the controls moved off the page and into the focused card the item view already uses: a floating card, the page dimmed and blurred behind it, rising from the bottom. The page stays a calm grid. Every phone is live: tap Shuffle or the hero to open the card, then use the locks, the bar and the chips. Tap outside the card or the cross to close it.

One pattern, not twoPieces open in a floating card. Shuffle would too, so the card means "focus on one thing" everywhere.
The page stays calmThe grid does not carry locks, undo and a bar. They appear only when you are shuffling.
One tap gives a resultTapping Shuffle on the page deals a fresh outfit as the card opens. You get a result and the controls together.
Closing keeps itWhatever you stopped on becomes the hero on the page, so nothing you liked is lost.
Version 1

Hero on the page, card to steer

The page keeps its hero outfit at the top. Tap the hero, or the floating Shuffle, to open the card. The hero is where you see the result and the card is where you steer.

GoodMatches your first idea: a hero at the top of both pages, tied to Shuffle. The hero is a compact preview, so the grid starts sooner than before.
Trade-offTwo places show the outfit, the hero and the card. They always agree, but there is a little duplication.
FavesThe card has no locks, since a fave is a finished outfit. The button reads Show another.
Version 2

No hero, popup only

The most minimal page. Just the grid and the floating Shuffle. The card is the only place a single outfit is shown large.

GoodLeast to build and the calmest page. The grid shows the most outfits at once.
Trade-offThe page loses the hero you asked for, and closing the card leaves no trace of what you dealt. You would need to fave it to keep it.
Version 3, my pick

Version 1 with lenses inside the card

The lens chips live in the card, beside the locks, because that is where you are deciding what to look at. The page behind stays free of chips. The card has a line saying why you are seeing this outfit, and the grid follows whichever lens you leave on.

Why this oneIt fixes the unfiltered All page without adding anything to it. Discovery is a choice you make inside the card, and the page is the calm result.
FavesThe chips there are your fave levels, so Show another can stay within Love or All-time.
NeedsOnly data the app has: fave levels, when a piece was added and which pieces are in your faves.

In the real app the card would also unfold to full screen when scrolled, like the item card does. It is left fixed here.
