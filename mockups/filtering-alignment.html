4 · Recommendation

Use B everywhere, built once

Put the types on their own line under the tabs, and make that one shared component that every picker and browser uses. It keeps the top-level tabs where your thumb expects them, it never scrolls them away, and it costs one extra row only when there is something to choose.

  • Same shape on every screenTabs, then types beneath. Choose a tab to open its types; tap a type again to clear it.
  • Same visibility ruleShow types only when you own pieces in two or more. Always A to Z.
  • Same empty ruleHide categories with nothing in them everywhere. The wardrobe can keep faded shelves only if that is a deliberate choice.
  • Types never leakChanging tab clears the type, as the capsule editor does now.

Order of work

  1. Extract the row from the capsule editor into a shared helper that takes the pool of pieces and the draft's tab and type.
  2. Outfit builder and filter sheet piece pages next. They are the same job and the same large grids.
  3. Wardrobe after that. It has the most room for a second row, but it also has the view toggle and archive tab to fit around.
  4. Accessories picker picks it up automatically and shows a second row only if a shelf grows past one type.
  5. Leave search and the tag, fave and capsule chips alone. They filter by something other than category.

Design C is worth keeping in mind if the wardrobe ever needs more than types, such as colour or brand. For one extra level it adds a tap and hides the choice.
